CareConn

Ask for a little help. Give a little help. Right where you live.

Why we exist

Asking for help is hard. Offering it is hard too, because you don't know who needs it. CareConn simply brings the two together — neighbour to neighbour, without money or paperwork.

Who you can be

Seeker

Watch over users and requests so the community stays healthy.

Seeking help

1Create an account and add your location.
2Post what you need, in your own words.
3A volunteer nearby accepts.
4It's done — leave a rating if you like.

Volunteering

1Create an account and add your skills.
2Browse open requests near you.
3Accept, help, and mark it complete.
4Build trust through ratings.

What we believe

People deserve to ask without shame. Volunteers deserve to choose freely. Help should be local, personal, and free. Nothing here costs money, and no one is forced to accept.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No payments, ever.

Can I be both?

One role per account. Make a second account if you need both.

Who chooses the volunteer?

We suggest someone suitable. They still choose to accept.

What if no one accepts?

Your request stays open. You can edit it or post again.
